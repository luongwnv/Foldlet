Foldlet 0.11.1 adds app notification badges and smooths the cover notification list.

- Apps with active notifications show a red badge with a white count at the top-right of their drawer icon. Counts update without rebuilding the grid or changing its scroll position. Badges disappear when notifications are cleared and show `99+` above 99.
- Badges require Notification access, but work independently of the Samsung notification override. Counts represent active notification cards, rather than an app’s private unread-message total. Foldlet’s own service notification is excluded.
- Group summaries are hidden when individual notifications from the same package and group are available. Summary-only notifications are retained. The list and drawer counts use the same grouping rule.
- Existing notification cards keep their positions while the list is open. Newly arriving cards are appended. Reopening sorts newest first. Updates reuse unchanged cards and preserve the visible scroll anchor.
- On Android 14+, leaving Foldlet notifications keeps Foldlet covering Samsung until the native clock has settled. Samsung’s notification page is not revealed during that return. If the clock cannot be reached, Foldlet restores its notification panel and reports the problem.
- The temporary cover renders only Foldlet’s own UI in memory and is released after the transition, or when the cover turns off, the phone locks, or the phone unfolds. No other app screenshots or notification history are saved.

Verified on the Galaxy Z Flip3 (SM-F711B): with Samsung’s native notification page behind Foldlet, the Foldlet cover remained attached until the clock was ready. The final APK also returned from the direct notification gesture to the clock. Two individual Samsung Routines notifications are counted once each, excluding their group summary. Release build, Android lint, and five focused notification policy checks passed.

Install **Foldlet-0.11.1.apk** over the existing version and open Foldlet once. Grant Notification access for badges and cover notifications. **Foldlet Controls 1.1.0** is unchanged. USB is needed only for forced rotation. Both APKs and SHA-256 files are attached.
