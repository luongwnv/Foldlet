Foldlet 0.10.3 adds sensor-driven Auto rotation for the Galaxy Z Flip3 cover screen.

- Choose **Auto (sensor)** in App orientation or the drawer rotation menu. Turn the folded phone to rotate the running cover-screen app in all four directions.
- Corrects the cover panel's 180-degree offset from the device sensor, calibrated on an SM-F711B.
- Uses a short settling delay and ignores flat/diagonal positions to reduce unwanted rotation.
- **App default** keeps the previous automatic mode's behavior: the app and Samsung choose their normal orientation. Existing preferences are preserved.
- Per-app orientation includes **Use general setting**, **Auto (sensor)**, and **App default**, alongside the existing fixed portrait/landscape actions. Reopening an app from Foldlet Recent also applies its orientation choice.
- Auto stops and restores the cover rotation policy when returning to Samsung Home, turning the screen off, unfolding, locking, or stopping Foldlet. It leaves the inner display and system auto-rotate switch unchanged.
- Updates the USB helper protocol and reconnects after a temporary helper failure while USB remains connected. The computer-owned heartbeat still expires when the connection is lost; the app cannot keep the privileged helper alive by itself.

**USB is still required for forced rotation**, including Auto (sensor). No root or Shizuku is required. After installing the APK, open Foldlet once and restart `scripts/adb_rotation_bridge.py` from this version of the repository. Hold the cover upright while turning it; when flat, it keeps the last direction.

Verified on an SM-F711B running Android 14 / One UI 6.1.1: the user confirmed upright content with the cameras at the left, top, and bottom. Stopping the computer helper restored the cover policy to its original free/default state and disabled the sensor session; inner rotation and the system auto-rotate switch remained unchanged. The release build, Android lint, and six focused rotation-policy checks passed.

Update **Foldlet-0.10.3.apk** over the existing app. **Foldlet Controls 1.1.0** is unchanged. Both signed, non-debuggable beta APKs and their SHA-256 files are attached.
