Foldlet 0.11.0 adds cover-screen notification opening and expands the app drawer.

- **Override Samsung notifications** replaces Samsung’s cover notification list when enabled, with Notification access granted and the cover unlocked. Swipe right from the Samsung clock to open Foldlet directly before Samsung’s notification pager moves, then tap a notification card. The list has no title or Back button.
- Opens an activity notification link on the cover screen, preserving the app’s original PendingIntent. If that link cannot be used, Foldlet opens the app launcher on the cover and reports the fallback. Exact conversations depend on the app’s link and Android display restrictions.
- The list refreshes as notifications arrive or disappear, retains scroll position, and returns to Samsung’s clock using an edge swipe. Disabling the feature or revoking notification access restores Samsung’s list. Widget pages retain Samsung controls.
- Notification content remains on device and is not saved. Pending opens are canceled when leaving the flow, turning the cover off, or stopping Foldlet. Auto-cancel notifications are dismissed only after the target app is confirmed on the cover.
- The drawer now includes **Foldlet itself** and system apps such as **Settings**. It combines LauncherApps with MAIN/LAUNCHER, INFO, and LEANBACK launcher discovery, skips invalid activities individually, and offers an explicit **All apps** toolbar button. User-hidden apps and Favorites/Recent filters remain available.
- Fixes right-edge navigation inside Foldlet and Samsung Settings. Android 14+ uses an accessibility display surface for navigation and for menus opened over Settings, where Samsung hides ordinary app overlays. The sidebar opens on gesture release so the same swipe cannot dismiss it.
- Other clock gestures are forwarded to Samsung. Widget pages keep their original controls.
- Includes English and Vietnamese settings and messages.

Verified on the SM-F711B: Samsung’s notification page was detected and replaced. A local test notification opened its original `test-thread` content on display 1. Foldlet and Settings were both found through the drawer search. Right-edge navigation worked in Foldlet and Samsung Settings, including opening the drawer over Settings. The direct notification gesture opened Foldlet while Samsung stayed on its clock. A left swipe from the clock still reached Samsung’s native widget page. Release build and Android lint passed.

Install **Foldlet-0.11.0.apk** over the existing version and open Foldlet once. Grant Notification access for the override. **Foldlet Controls 1.1.0** is unchanged. USB is needed only for forced rotation. Both APKs and SHA-256 files are attached.
